Arrival orientation

Two facilities, one island day

Cruise documents name your arrival facility. Mahogany Bay is a purpose-built cruise centre; Coxen Hole sits nearer town markets. Transfer times to West Bay and wildlife parks vary with traffic - confirm pickup with your operator for the pier you actually use.
