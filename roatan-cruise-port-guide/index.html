Roatan is a pier port with more than one cruise facility. Calls typically run 7-10 hours. Confirm whether you arrive at Mahogany Bay or Coxen Hole before locking a pickup point.

Cruise Passenger Snapshot

Typical Time In Port
7-10 hours (typical)
Best For
Orienting at Mahogany Bay or Coxen Hole before west-side plans
Walking Required
Minimal inside terminals; taxis to town and west side
Family Friendly
Strong with age-appropriate picks
Return To Ship Friendly
Build your own buffer; confirm operator return plan
Popular Excursion Types
Confirm facility, then beach, reef or park plans

Where ships arrive

Mahogany Bay

Purpose-built cruise centre. Confirm meeting points inside the complex with your operator.

Coxen Hole

Town pier with different layout and traffic. Do not assume the same taxi rank as Mahogany Bay.
