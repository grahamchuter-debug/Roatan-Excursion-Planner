Wildlife park visits are a popular Roatan shore option. Licensed parks may offer supervised sloth time and monkey observation under staff rules. Treat encounters as chance-based and confirm whether handling is allowed on the day you visit.

  • ✓Morning departures leave room for an afternoon beach if the call is long enough
  • ✓Follow staff instructions - welfare rules apply and can change
  • ✓Wear closed shoes for garden paths; bring cash for optional extras
  • ✓Confirm pickup for Mahogany Bay or Coxen Hole - do not guess the facility
