About Roatan Excursion Planner

Roatan sits on the Mesoamerican Reef with West Bay sand and wildlife parks a transfer away from south-coast cruise facilities. This site exists to make reef, beach and logistics trade-offs clear before you spend the call.

We write for passengers weighing snorkel boats against West Bay, and for guests who need honest language about Mahogany Bay versus Coxen Hole. We are not a cruise line, ticket marketplace or port authority.

Ship schedules here are synced from the Caribbean Shore Excursions authority import for Roatan only. Arrival and departure times can still change; confirm with your cruise line.
