On a typical Roatan call, many guests choose between a reef / snorkel day and a West Bay beach day. Both can be excellent. They spend hours differently.

Reef boats maximise underwater time with gear and a guide. Beach days maximise swimming, shade and optional shore snorkel without a fixed boat itinerary.

Reef day vs beach day

Reef / snorkel day

  • Boat trips toward Mesoamerican Reef sites with supplied gear
  • Better when underwater time matters more than loungers
  • Visibility and marine life vary with weather and season
  • Confirm return window in writing with the operator

West Bay beach day

  • Sand, swimming and optional shore snorkel near resorts
  • Better when shade and flexible pacing matter
  • Transfer time from either facility depends on traffic
  • Pair with a morning park visit only if the call is long enough
